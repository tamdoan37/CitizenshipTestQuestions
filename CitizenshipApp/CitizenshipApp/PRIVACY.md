# Privacy Policy — Liberty Civics

**Last updated: September 25, 2026**

Liberty Civics ("the app") is a free study aid for the U.S. citizenship (civics)
test. Your privacy matters, and this policy explains — in plain language — what
the app does and does not do with your information.

## The short version

**The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information.** There
are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no trackers, and no analytics. Everything
you do stays on your device.

## What is stored, and where

The app saves the following **only on your device** (using the operating
system's local storage). It is never uploaded to us or anyone else:

- Your first name and home state (from the setup screen)
- Your study progress, quiz history, and the answers you've pinned or marked
- Your settings (voice, speed, reminder preferences)

Because this data lives only on your device, we cannot see it, and it is removed
if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Permissions

- **Notifications (optional):** If you turn on the daily study reminder, the app
  schedules a local notification on your device. These reminders are generated
  on your device and are not sent through us or any server.
- **Text‑to‑speech:** Read‑aloud and Listen Mode use your device's built‑in
  speech engine. No audio is recorded and nothing is sent anywhere.

The app does **not** request access to your microphone, camera, contacts,
location, or photos.

## Network use

The app works fully offline. If a study‑content update is configured, the app
may download a small public JSON file of current government officials (for
example, your governor and senators). This is an ordinary download of public
information and sends none of your data.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including
children. Since no data is collected at all, it is safe for all ages.

## Third parties

We do not use advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or any third‑party service
that would receive your information.

## Not affiliated with the government

Liberty Civics is an independent study aid and is **not affiliated with or
endorsed by USCIS or the U.S. government**. Always confirm current test answers
at [uscis.gov](https://www.uscis.gov/citizenship).
